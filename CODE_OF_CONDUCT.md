# Code of Conduct

DA3-CAD is committed to a welcoming, harassment-free collaboration space.

Participants should be respectful, assume good faith while evaluating evidence,
accept constructive technical criticism, acknowledge sources and contributions,
and avoid discriminatory, sexualized, threatening, or personally demeaning
conduct. Scientific disagreement is welcome; attacks on people are not.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when conduct harms the community. Enforcement decisions
should be proportionate, documented privately, and protect reporters from
retaliation.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact
method on the GitHub profile rather than opening a public issue. Include links,
dates, and enough context to investigate. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical.

This policy is informed by the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
